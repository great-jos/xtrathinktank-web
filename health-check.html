The business health check

BUSINESS HEALTH CHECK FORM

Think of us as your business health checker.

Purpose: This assessment helps us understand the current health of your business, identify areas of risk, and highlight opportunities for improvement and growth.

Instructions: Please choose one answer for each question. Your score and result will be shown as soon as you submit.

1. BUSINESS INFORMATION

Years in business
Number of employees
Current business stage

2. FINANCIAL HEALTH

Maximum 24 points

Do you keep proper and up-to-date financial records?
Do you know your actual monthly profit?
Do you separate personal and business finances?
Do you monitor your cash flow regularly?
Do you prepare regular financial reports?
Do you know which products/services are most profitable?
Do you regularly review your business expenses?
Do you have a cash-flow forecast or financial budget?

3. TAX & COMPLIANCE

Maximum 18 points

Is your business properly registered?
Are your tax filings up to date?
Are your statutory obligations properly managed?
Do you maintain proper documentation for tax purposes?
Do you have a system for monitoring compliance deadlines?
Do you have professional support for tax/compliance matters?

4. BUSINESS STRATEGY

Maximum 18 points

Does your business have clear goals?
Do you have a written business/growth plan?
Do you have measurable KPIs?
Do you regularly review business performance?
Do you know what is currently limiting your growth?
Do you have a clear plan for the next 12 months?

5. SALES & CUSTOMERS

Maximum 18 points

Do you consistently track your sales?
Do you know your most profitable products/services?
Do you track customer retention/repeat purchases?
Do you have a clear customer acquisition strategy?
Do you regularly collect customer feedback?
Do you know why customers choose or leave your business?

6. PEOPLE & OPERATIONS

Maximum 21 points

Are employees' roles clearly defined?
Do employees have measurable performance targets?
Do you regularly review employee performance?
Are your major business processes documented?
Can your business operate effectively without you?
Do you have adequate HR policies and procedures?
Do you have the right skills and people to support growth?

7. RISK & INTERNAL CONTROLS

Maximum 18 points

Are your bank accounts regularly reconciled?
Do you have controls over business spending?
Are financial responsibilities properly separated?
Do you maintain adequate transaction records?
Do you monitor business risks regularly?
Do you have a business continuity plan?

8. TECHNOLOGY & SYSTEMS

Maximum 15 points

Do you use accounting/business management software?
Are important business processes automated?
Are your business records securely stored?
Do your systems provide reliable information for decision-making?
Do you regularly review opportunities for technology improvement?

9. BUSINESS OWNER'S ASSESSMENT

Not scored. Helps us understand your priorities.

What is currently your biggest business challenge?
