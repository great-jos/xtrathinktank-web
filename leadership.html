Oke Oladimeji

Managing Executive

MBA · ACTI · ACA · PGD · MIPMAN · MNIM · HND

Oke Oladimeji is the Managing Executive of XtraThinkTank Solutions Ltd, with over 15 years of professional experience spanning accounting, taxation, financial management, business development and management consulting. He is a Chartered Accountant and Chartered Tax Practitioner with a strong passion for business growth, innovation and human development.

As a business solution and support expert, he is passionate about helping businesses identify challenges, develop practical solutions, strengthen their financial structures and achieve sustainable growth. He is also an author, entrepreneur, creative thinker and advocate for SME development.

He is the author of The Complete SME Guide, hosts a monthly Instagram Live session, SME Hangout, and is the convener of the annual Business Financial Conference — initiatives through which he shares practical knowledge and supports entrepreneurs building stronger businesses.
